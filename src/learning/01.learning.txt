vi. jiske pass state , action dono hai . or usme hmko jo jo kaam krwana hai ki yese ho to ye krna or ye ho to ye krna ke liye multiple fun banayenge . us fun me hm bataye hai ki jab v ye fun call ho to ye state me ka ke ye action perform krna .

1. user/fetchUser/pending
2.user/fetchUser/fullfilled
3.user/fetchUser/rejected

to hmko extra reducer ke ander addBuilder milta hai. uske through hm addCase me jate hai or teeno case hone pe kya hoga wo set krte hai jisme meri madat state, action krta hai.

      state.loading = true;
      state.error = null;
    }) 

    *** addBuilder hamara helper obj hota hai.

kuch yese , addBuilder me addCase me gaye waha pahele case ko access kiye usme state , action mila waha ham state.loading, state.error set kr diye . oe ye hm loading, error apne initial state me bna lenge palene set krne se. issi me fetchUser.fullfilled wale case me data set krne ke liye state.user me action.payload pass karenge.
